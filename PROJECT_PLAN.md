# Dastarkhwan Website Structure Plan

## Page 1: index.html (Homepage)
**Purpose**: Grand entrance, showcase highlights, drive conversions

### Sections:
1. ✅ **Hero** (DONE)
   - Full-viewport height (100vh)
   - Background: Traditional Nawabi courtyard image
   - Dark overlay for text readability
   - Heading: "Experience the Royal Flavors of Bahawalpur"
   - Subtext: "A century-old tradition of culinary excellence, served with love."
   - CTA Button: "Reserve a Table" (links to visit.html)

2. ✅ **Heritage Teaser** (DONE)
   - Heading: "Our Heritage"
   - Brief description of the 100-year-old haveli
   - Button: "Read Our Full Story" (links to about.html)

3. **🆕 Signature Dishes**
   - Heading: "Our Culinary Masterpieces"
   - 3-4 featured dishes in a grid layout
   - Each dish: Image, name, brief description, price
   - Button: "View Full Menu" (links to products.html)

4. ** The Dining Experience**
   - Heading: "An Unforgettable Experience"
   - 3-4 feature highlights (icons + text):
     - "Authentic Nawabi Ambiance"
     - "Live Traditional Music" (Thu-Sat)
     - "Courtyard Dining"
     - "Private Dining Rooms"
   - Background: Alternating color or subtle pattern

5. ** Testimonials**
   - Heading: "What Our Guests Say"
   - 2-3 customer quotes in cards
   - Star ratings, customer names, dates
   - Carousel or grid layout

6. **🆕 Visit Us Teaser**
   - Heading: "Plan Your Visit"
   - Address (fictional Bahawalpur location)
   - Opening hours
   - Phone number
   - Button: "Get Directions & Hours" (links to visit.html)
   - Background: Dark burgundy with white text

7. ** Footer**
   - Restaurant name & logo
   - Quick links (Home, About, Menu, Visit)
   - Contact info (address, phone, email)
   - Social media icons (placeholder)
   - Copyright notice
   - Background: Dark slate or burgundy

## Page 2: about.html (Our Story)
**Purpose**: Build emotional connection, establish authenticity

### Sections:
1. **Hero (Smaller)**
   - Heading: "Our Story" or "A Century of Excellence"
   - Subheading about the haveli's history
   - Background: Architectural detail of the building
   - Height: 50-60vh (smaller than homepage hero)

2. **The Building**
   - Heading: "Our Historic Haveli"
   - 2-column layout: Text + Image
   - Details about the 100+ year old architecture
   - Mention Bahawalpur's royal heritage
   - Features: Carved arches, jali screens, courtyard

3. **Our Philosophy**
   - Heading: "Culinary Philosophy"
   - Commitment to authentic Nawabi cuisine
   - Traditional recipes passed down generations
   - Use of local, fresh ingredients
   - Full-width background image or pattern

4. **The Team** (Optional)
   - Heading: "Meet Our Culinary Masters"
   - Chef profile(s) with photos
   - Brief bios highlighting expertise
   - Grid or card layout

5. **Awards & Recognition** (Optional)
   - Heading: "Accolades"
   - List of awards, features, or press mentions
   - Logo badges or simple text list

6. **Image Gallery**
   - Heading: "Inside Dastarkhwan"
   - 6-8 photos in a masonry or grid layout
   - Categories: Architecture, Food, Ambiance, Events
   - Lightbox effect on click (optional enhancement)

7. **CTA Section**
   - Heading: "Experience It Yourself"
   - Button: "Reserve a Table" (links to visit.html)
   - Button: "View Our Menu" (links to products.html)

## Page 3: products.html (Menu)
**Purpose**: Showcase offerings, drive reservations

### Sections:
1. **Hero (Smaller)**
   - Heading: "Our Menu"
   - Subheading: "Authentic Nawabi Cuisine"
   - Background: Beautiful food photography
   - Height: 50-60vh

2. **Menu Introduction**
   - Brief text about the culinary tradition
   - Note about spice levels, dietary options
   - "Download PDF Menu" button

3. **Menu Categories** (Tabs or Accordion)
   
   **a. Starters & Appetizers**
   - 4-6 items
   - Each: Name, description, price, spice indicator
   
   **b. Main Courses (Nawabi Specialties)**
   - 6-8 signature dishes
   - Highlight most popular items
   - Include: Bahawalpuri Biryani, Royal Korma, Kebabs
   
   **c. Breads & Rice**
   - 4-5 items (Naan, Roti, Pulao, etc.)
   
   **d. Desserts**
   - 4-5 traditional sweets
   - Kheer, Gulab Jamun, etc.
   
   **e. Beverages**
   - Traditional drinks (Lassi, Sherbet)
   - Tea/Coffee
   - Soft drinks

4. **Signature Dishes Spotlight**
   - Heading: "Chef's Recommendations"
   - 3 dishes with larger images and detailed descriptions
   - "Must-try" badges

5. **Dietary Information**
   - Icons for: Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Spice Levels
   - Allergen information note

6. **CTA Section**
   - Heading: "Ready to Dine?"
   - Button: "Make a Reservation" (links to visit.html)
   - Phone number for orders

## Page 4: visit.html (Plan Your Visit)
**Purpose**: Provide practical information, reduce friction

### Sections:
1. **Hero (Smaller)**
   - Heading: "Visit Us"
   - Subheading: "We'd Love to Welcome You"
   - Background: Welcoming exterior or courtyard image
   - Height: 50-60vh

2. **Location & Hours**
   - 2-column layout:
   
   **Left Column - Address:**
   - Full fictional address in Bahawalpur
   - Embedded Google Map (iframe)
   - "Get Directions" button
   
   **Right Column - Hours:**
   - Opening hours table:
     - Lunch: 12:00 PM - 3:30 PM
     - Dinner: 7:00 PM - 11:00 PM
     - Days of operation
   - Special hours (holidays, Ramadan)

3. **Reservations**
   - Heading: "Reserve a Table"
   - Phone number (prominent)
   - Email address
   - Simple booking form (optional):
     - Name
     - Date
     - Time
     - Number of guests
     - Special requests
     - Submit button
   - Note: "For large parties, please call us directly"

4. **Getting Here**
   - Heading: "How to Reach Us"
   - Parking information
   - Public transport options
   - Ride-share pickup points
   - Landmark references

5. **Private Events & Catering**
   - Heading: "Host Your Event With Us"
   - Private dining room info
   - Wedding/catering services
   - Contact button for inquiries

6. **FAQ**
   - Heading: "Frequently Asked Questions"
   - Accordion or list format:
     - "What is the dress code?"
     - "Do you accommodate children?"
     - "Is there wheelchair access?"
     - "Do you take credit cards?"
     - "Can I bring my own cake?"

7. **Contact CTA**
   - Heading: "Still Have Questions?"
   - Phone, email, social media links
   - "We're Here to Help" message

### Header/Navigation:
- ✅ Fixed position

### Footer (All Pages):
- Restaurant name & tagline
- Quick navigation links
- Contact information

- ✅ Color palette (burgundy, gold, cream)

- ✅ Container pattern for content width
